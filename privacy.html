HuatLui Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

HuatLui is an Android investment portfolio tracking app. This policy describes how the app currently works and areas that need review when third-party SDKs or features change.

Portfolio information

Information you enter, including brokerage accounts, transactions, dividends, holdings, price targets, investment notes and preferences, is stored locally on your device. Core portfolio tracking does not require a cloud account.

Market prices and FX

The app requests market prices and exchange rates from external services. These requests may reveal your IP address and the requested stock symbols to the respective service providers. Their privacy policies apply to processing on their services.

Advertising

The Android project may contain Google Mobile Ads SDK integration. When ads are enabled, Google and partners may process identifiers, usage data and other technical information for advertising, fraud prevention and measurement. Please see Google's privacy policy. The publisher must confirm the final app's SDK behavior, required consent and Google Play Data safety declarations before enabling live ads.

Backup and exports

Exported JSON portfolio backups can contain sensitive financial data. You control where you save and share them. Keep backup files secure and delete copies you no longer require.

Data sharing

HuatLui does not offer a feature to sell your manually entered portfolio records. Network market-data providers, advertising services (if enabled) and platform backup services have their own data processing practices.

Your choices

You can edit and delete entries inside HuatLui. You can remove local app data through Android settings or by uninstalling. Export a backup before clearing anything you wish to retain. System and advertising preferences can be managed using Android settings.

Children

HuatLui is designed for personal investment tracking and is not directed to children.

Questions

Visit the support page for questions. A verified publisher contact address will be included before public release.
