FAQ

Good questions before getting started.

Does HuatLui connect directly to a brokerage?

HuatLui works from records you enter or import. Automatic broker connection is not currently advertised.

Will HuatLui tell me which stock to buy?

It helps you organize and review your investments. Statuses such as Hold and Add Candidate are selected by you, not generated investment recommendations.

Do price alerts send push notifications?

No. Alerts appear inside the app when stored prices meet your thresholds. Quotes can be stale or delayed.

Do what-if simulations place real orders?

No. They are calculations only and do not write trades or submit orders.

Where can I download HuatLui?

The Android app is being prepared for Google Play. The official listing will be linked when available.
